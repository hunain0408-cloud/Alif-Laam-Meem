The Bridal Journey

How Our Bespoke Bridal Process Works

Whether you visit us in Landhi Town, Karachi or book a virtual video consultation from anywhere in Pakistan, we make your bridal journey effortless.

Step 01

One-on-One Consultation

Connect with our bridal head at 0322-1200965 to select your Barat or Walima design, customize shade cards (crimson, ruby, ivory, pistachio, champagne), and finalize fabric swatches.

Step 02

Adda Handwork & Progress Updates

Our master karigars begin hand-embellishing your ensemble with zardozi, kora, dabka, and crystals. We share live WhatsApp video updates of your khaka and embroidery stages.

Step 03

Couture Fitting & Insured Delivery

Stitched with can-can volume petticoats, pure raw silk linings, and finished dupattas—packed in a signature Alif Laam Meem bridal box and delivered safely across Pakistan.
