Our Brand Story

Authentic Pakistani Elegance, Stitched to Perfection

Named after the sacred Arabic letters Alif Laam Meem (الم), our fashion house was founded in Landhi Town, Karachi to bring genuine, boutique-grade Pakistani designer suits and bespoke custom tailoring to women across Pakistan.

Every ensemble—from our breathable Summer Chikankari Lawn and Winter 9000 Micro-Velvet shawls to our heirloom Barat and Walima bridal lehengas—is crafted in-house by veteran Pakistani karigars and master tailors.
